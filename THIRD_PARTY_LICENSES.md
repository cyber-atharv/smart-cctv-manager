## 2. Research & Algorithmic Acknowledgments

Universal Smart Vision draws algorithmic insights and architectural references from several prominent open-source computer vision surveillance implementations:

1. **Enhanced Smart Surveillance** (`payaldongre/EnhancedSmartSurveillance`): Insights into multi-camera surveillance workflows.
2. **AI Surveillance & Anomaly Detection** (`shreekorde/Ai-surveillance-anomaly-detection`): Insights into crowd density estimation and loitering boundaries.
3. **Intelligent Defence Surveillance System** (`samarth3394/Surveillance_system`): Insights into perimeter tripwires and threat condition scoring.
4. **AI-Powered Multi-Camera Surveillance** (`wSubham/AI-Weapon-Detection-and-Multi-Camera-Surveillance`): Reference concepts for weapon bounding box tags and tracking.
5. **Intelligent CCTV Video Analytics** (`Prarabdha14/SmartSurveillance`): Multi-object tracking and entry/exit counting.
6. **CCTV Video Anomaly Detection** (`saadkhan2003/CCTV_Video_Anomaly_Detection`): OpenVINO / edge anomaly detection concepts.

All visual templates, styling, biomechanical invariants, UI components, and domain routing have been independently engineered by the authors for Universal Smart Vision.
